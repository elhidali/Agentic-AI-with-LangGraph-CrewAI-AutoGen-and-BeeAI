### 4. ALTERNATIVE INGREDIENTS (to stay within budget & meet dietary needs)

| Original Ingredient | Alternative | When to Use | Approx. Cost Impact |
|---------------------|-------------|-------------|---------------------|
| Boneless skinless chicken breast | **Firm tofu**, pressed & cubed (14‑oz block) | Vegetarian version; still low‑sodium if no added salt | $1.80‑$2.20 per block (saves $0.80‑$1.20) |
| Boneless skinless chicken breast | **Canned low‑sodium chicken**, drained & rinsed (2 × 5‑oz cans) | When fresh chicken is pricey or unavailable | $1.50 total (saves $1.50) |
| Fresh broccoli/snap peas | **Stir‑fry frozen vegetable mix** (broccoli, carrots, snap peas, 12‑oz bag) | Saves prep time and often cheaper | $1.00‑$1.30 per bag (similar cost) |
| Brown rice | **Quinoa** (if on sale) or **cauliflower rice** (for lower carb) | Quinoa: use ½ cup dry (~$1.20); Cauliflower rice: $2.00/head (yields ~4 cups) | Still within budget; may slightly increase or decrease cost |
| Sesame oil | **Toasted sesame seeds** (1 tsp sprinkled at end) | If oil is out; adds nut‑free flavor | $2.00 per 4‑oz jar (lasts many uses) |
| Low‑sodium soy sauce | **Regular soy sauce diluted 1:1 with water** | Only if low‑sodium unavailable; cuts sodium by half | Same price as regular soy sauce |
| Low‑sodium oyster sauce | **Regular oyster sauce used sparingly** (½ Tbsp) + water to make 1 Tbsp | When low‑sodium version not found; reduces sodium intake | Same price; use less |

*All alternatives keep the recipe nut‑free and can be adjusted to maintain low‑sodium levels.*

### 5. QUICK PREPARATION REMINDER  

1. **Marinate** chicken (or tofu) in 1 Tbsp low‑sodium soy sauce + 1 tsp cornstarch for 10 min.  
2. **Heat** vegetable oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Add garlic & ginger; stir‑fry 30 sec.  
3. **Add** chicken; cook until browned (≈4‑5 min). Remove and set aside.  
4. **Toss in** bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas; stir‑fry 3‑4 min until crisp‑tender.  
5. **Return** chicken to pan; add remaining soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, and water; stir to coat and heat through (1‑2 min).  
6. **Serve** over hot cooked brown rice or quinoa. Garnish with sliced green onions.
